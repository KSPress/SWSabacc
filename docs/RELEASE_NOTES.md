SWU Sabacc 0.7.25

- Galactic Conquest is playable from PLAY. Choose a leader printing and an 8-, 16-, or 32-planet galaxy. Capture every planet to win.
- Fleets and reinforcement shipments travel hyperspace lanes in real time. Battles use the card engine, and destroyed cards leave their campaign rosters.
- Build specialized factories, planetary defenses, and reconnaissance. Commission additional leaders at four, eight, and twelve held planets.
- Conquest includes autosave and three named save slots. Battles use planet art where available and a distinct orbital backdrop elsewhere.
- Campaign decision cards deal from a center stack and reveal on an octagonal wood table.
- The Android APK uses version code 35 and the existing signing identity. Extract the Windows ZIP and keep the EXE and PCK together.
