SWU Sabacc 0.7.17

- Arena units use Sabacc-shaped art frames with combat stats, aspect-colored borders, and alignment lighting. Pilots and upgrades slide out from behind their host. Attachment previews crop to the illustration and the full cards are available in inspection.
- Attack-declaration and unit-entry abilities that trigger together can resolve in a chosen order.
- Added missing rules for cards including Relentless, Fett's Firespray, Boba Fett — Disintegrator, Occupation Officer, AT-AT Suppressor, Hyperspace Disaster, King Katuunko, and Summa-verminoth. Card rules work continues, and the inspector lists unsupported text.
- The Android APK uses version code 27 and the existing signing identity. Extract the Windows ZIP and keep the EXE and PCK together.
