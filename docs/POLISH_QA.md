# SWU Sabacc 0.5.0 visual polish validation

## Pack rewards and presentation

Packs retain all ten card slots and can add one extra cosmetic reward: a 12.5%
emblem chance or 5% scene-background chance. Those rolls are mutually exclusive.
The selected pool excludes cosmetics owned or reserved in other pending packs at
the time of the roll. If that pool is exhausted, no bonus is added. Existing
sealed packs retain their saved contents; upgrading does not reroll them.

Cosmetic rolls are saved when the pack is earned. Individual bonus reveals save
ownership immediately and cannot be repeated. Collect requires all card and bonus
reveals, preventing an unrevealed cosmetic from being discarded. Legacy packs
without bonus fields still open normally. An emblem includes access to its
matching hologram background through the existing profile selection system.

The pack has moving foil sheen, the equipped emblem, a responsive tear seam,
a short anticipation movement, separated wrapper halves, radial light/shard
bursts and staggered face-down cards. Rare/Legendary reveals use larger impact
bursts and rarity-colored borders. Incomplete rows are centered. Mouse dragging,
touch dragging and a tap/button alternative are supported. Rotation during the
card flight cancels stale destinations and lays the cards out in the new ratio.

## Emblem and datapad identity

All 25 glyphs were reviewed in `build/qa/polish/emblem_palettes.png`. Decorative
colors come from `game/data/emblem_palettes.json`, regenerated by
`tools/build_emblem_palettes.py`. Fett, Old Republic, Iron Crest and Death's Head
have visually reviewed overrides so a small saturated detail cannot outweigh
the main olive, ivory or silver artwork. Supplied original images are unchanged.

Emblem backdrops use those palettes for their dark base, scan beam, circuitry and
orbit traces. Profile badges use the same palette. Deck/aspect colors still tint
the gameplay controls; they no longer overwrite an emblem's background palette.

Hologram opacity uses seeded noise and occasional independently timed brief signal
drops rather than a repeating sine pulse. Cursor position and finger contact add
smoothed tilt/parallax. Touch release returns toward neutral. Input is observed
without accepting events, preserving the controls underneath. Performance mode
freezes the decorative animation and tilt.

The default datapad blends its traces and ambient wash between player and enemy
leader/base aspects. The transition lasts 0.45 seconds at normal animation speed
and scales with the game's animation-speed setting, finishing before the normal
AI thinking delay. It follows the active turn, rather than a card-choice prompt's
chooser, so an opponent's action keeps its color during the player's response.

## Responsive checks

Eight window ratios were exercised: 360×640, 540×1200, 768×1024, 1024×768,
1280×720, 1920×1080, 2560×1080 and 960×540. The corrected matrix covers 152 screen
states across menus/hubs, settings, customization, collection, decks/editor,
battle setup, Saga/Spotlight maps, packs/rewards, mulligan, live matches and card
inspection. The test asserts the actual window size so a coalesced OS resize
cannot silently reuse the previous ratio.

The baseline exposed 22 overlapping-button observations. Icon-only SabaccButtons
were receiving 50px text margins on both sides, forcing 56px controls to 100px.
Correcting those margins fixes the Log/Settings/PASS action row in both
orientations. PASS also resets its width after rotation. Deck, cosmetic and card
collection grids are centered, including partial collection pages. Refreshes
remove old items before measuring the replacement layout.

## Results and evidence

- Corrected ratio/palette/reward suite: **114 checks passed, 0 failed**;
  **0 out-of-bounds buttons and 0 detected button overlaps** in the 152 states.
- Separate visual/interaction suite: **80 passed, 0 failed**. Verifies all 25 palettes, finger tilt,
  irregular flicker, turn blending, saved bonus ownership, rarity distribution
  and centered grids in tablet, tall phone and ultrawide layouts.
- Existing progression regression: **50 checks passed, 0 failed** with optional
  bonus rewards included.
- GDScript compilation: **0 broken scripts**. Manifest validation: **24 passed**.

Reports/screenshots/logs are under `build/qa/polish/`. The bounded-layout result is
`bounds/layout_issues.json` (empty). Final presentation screenshots and logs are
under `visuals-final/` and `visuals-final.log`. The initial pack-flight metadata
warning was fixed; corrected suites report no runtime script errors.

Version 0.5.0 uses Android version code 6 and retains package/signing identity and
both updater manifests. Native builds bundle the cosmetic assets and palette
metadata. No physical Android device was connected: touch checks use Godot input
and viewport geometry; device safe areas, installation and real-device GPU/touch
performance still need device testing. These checks cover the listed ratios and
states, not every possible window size or transient animation frame.

This is a UI/reward update. Card-engine coverage remains incomplete as documented
in the previous campaign release; no full-rules completion claim is made.
